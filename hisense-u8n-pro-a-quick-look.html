Hisense U8N Pro – A Quick Look

    Charlotte September 3, 2026

    Specs

    Mini LED backlight with 3200 dimming zones
    Built‑in Xin‑chip® AI Picture Processor Pro
    Peak brightness: 3000 nits (stable)
    Color coverage: 98% of DCI‑P3
    Audio power: 97W with a 4.1.2‑channel sound system

    Ports

    4 USB ports (3 are USB 2.0, 1 is USB 3.0) 4 HDMI ports, all of them are HDMI 2.1

    There’s also a USB port on the front left corner – super handy if you want to plug in a USB drive without reaching around the back.

    Design & Sound

    The subwoofer is placed partially outside the main body. The speakers are built into the left and right sides of the TV, angled slightly forward, which makes the sound feel more direct and immersive. The speaker housings are made of metal, so they look and feel premium. The whole TV is only 42mm thick, and when mounted on the wall, it can sit as close as 5mm from the wall.

    Camera & AI Features

    You can attach a camera to the U8N Pro. Once it’s on, you get useful AI features like auto‑pause when you leave the room and a fitness mirror mode for workouts.


    Screen Quality

    The screen specs: 
    4K resolution at 144Hz
    Peak brightness: 3000 nits
    Color coverage: 98% DCI‑P3


    I tested the brightness during the day with plenty of sunlight coming in through the window, and it was more than enough – at max level, it was actually a bit dazzling.


    Sharpness, Contrast & Gradation

    I checked the display with three test patterns (for resolution, tonal gradation, and grayscale). The U8N Pro performed really well – text and fine details were crisp, mid‑tones looked smooth, and both black and white areas showed cleanly with no obvious crushing or clipping.



    Viewing Angle

    The TV has a 178‑degree viewing angle. Even when I watched from a very side angle, the colors and brightness hardly changed at all – it’s almost invisible to the eye. So no matter where you sit in the living room, the picture looks great.

    Gaming Performance

    The TV supports 3‑way CEC – once you connect a game console via HDMI, the TV and console can power on together automatically. The U8N Pro has four HDMI 2.1 ports, and two of them support 4K at 144Hz, which means it can handle PS5 or any other console gaming without any trouble.
