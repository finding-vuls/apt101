Vivo X300

    Emma September 9, 2026

    The 6.31‑inch AMOLED flat screen feels light and steady in one hand – nice and grippy.

    Outdoors in bright sunlight, the brightness is good enough; the display doesn't wash out to a dull grey.

    The camera handles low‑light scenes really well. When shooting portraits, facial lighting and skin tones look natural – no heavy smoothing that turns you into a plastic doll.

    The telephoto lens is just average though. Under heavy use, battery drains quickly and it gets noticeably warm, which does hurt the overall experience. Speaker quality is nothing special either.
